Tickwell Privacy Policy

Effective 11 November 2026 · Applies to the Tickwell: Habit Tracker app for Android (com.mohit5899.tickwell)

In short: Tickwell does not collect, send or sell any personal data. Your habits stay on your phone. There are no accounts, ads, analytics or trackers.

What the app stores

Tickwell saves the habits you create (names, icons, colors, goals, reminder times) and the days you mark them done. This information is stored only in the app's private storage on your device. It is never sent to us or to anyone else.

What we collect

Nothing. Tickwell has no server, no user accounts, no advertising and no analytics or crash-reporting services. We cannot see your habits or anything else about how you use the app.

Reminders

If you turn on reminders, Tickwell schedules notifications locally on your phone using Android's notification system. No data leaves your device to do this.

Backups

You can choose to export a backup file. Tickwell creates the file on your device and opens Android's share sheet; you decide where it goes (for example Google Drive, email or your Files app). Once you share it, that file is handled by the app or service you chose, under its own privacy policy. Restoring reads a backup file you pick; it is not uploaded anywhere.

Permissions

  • Notifications – to show the habit reminders you set.
  • Run at startup – so scheduled reminders are kept after your phone restarts.
  • Vibration – for light haptic feedback when you tick a habit.
  • Internet – included by the app framework. Tickwell does not use it to send your data.

Deleting your data

Delete any habit inside the app, or uninstall Tickwell to remove all of its data from your device. Backup files you exported are not affected; delete them wherever you saved them.

Children

Tickwell is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. If the app ever starts collecting data, this policy will be updated before that happens.
